## 1. Project Overview
RetailSphere is a leading retail company operating across India. It sells products through three sales channels: Mobile Application, Website, and Physical Retail Stores. It is rapidly expanding in 25+ states and 300+ stores with millions of customers. But the company uses a legacy reporting system. Due to this, the numbers in the reports are different among the departments. So the company wants to modernise its enterprise analytics platform.

## 2. Business Problem
The company uses a reporting system that has so many drawbacks. Due to that, departments use different databases, inconsistent metric definitions, and different reports. The company does not have a single source of truth, resulting in inconsistent reports and conflicting business metrics across departments. Initially, the company operated at a smaller scale with fewer customers, lower sales volume, and simpler reporting requirements. But now the company is rapidly growing. So the company wants to modernise its enterprise analytics platform. So that every department uses the same database and generates correct numbers and faster and more reliable reporting that help the stakeholders make the right Business decisions.

## 3. Objectives
- Improve reporting accuracy
- Reduce manual reporting effort
- Build scalable data pipelines
- Centralise business data
